# Revision Engine

> **Product:** ArivuVazhi (அறிவுவழி) — AI-powered government exam preparation platform

## Inputs
- Mistakes
- Last review date
- Accuracy
- Topic difficulty
- Repeated errors
- Recent performance

## Initial Schedule
Example:
Day 0 → Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 15 → Day 30

The schedule can later become adaptive.

## Output
- Revision queue
- Priority
- Questions/concepts
- Completion state
