## Hierarchy
Exam
→ Subject
→ Topic
→ Sub-topic

## Exam Configuration
- duration
- totalQuestions
- totalMarks
- negativeMarking
- questionTypes
- subjectWeightage
- difficultyDistribution
- language
- version/effective date
