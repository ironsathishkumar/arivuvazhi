# AI Agents

> **Product:** ArivuVazhi (அறிவுவழி) — AI-powered government exam preparation platform

## Tutor Agent
Explains questions and concepts using retrieved knowledge.

## Question Generator
Creates questions from approved knowledge.

## Question Validator
Checks factual support, answer uniqueness, difficulty, ambiguity and source alignment.

## Mistake Analyzer
Classifies mistakes and recommends remediation.

## Study Planner
Creates daily/weekly plans from deterministic learning metrics.

## Revision Planner
Prioritizes topics/questions due for revision.

## Mock Analyst
Explains mock-test performance and identifies patterns.

## Performance Analyst
Turns objective metrics into understandable insights.

## Confidence Coach
Communicates evidence-based progress without predicting exam outcomes.

## Current Affairs Agent
Transforms approved current information into summaries, revision points and questions.
